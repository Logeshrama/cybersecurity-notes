# Datacom Cyber Security Operations

## Overview

Completed the Datacom Cyber Security Operations Virtual Experience Programme through Forage.

## What I Learned

### Incident Investigation

- Investigated a simulated ransomware incident.
- Identified attack vectors and indicators of compromise.

### Indicators of Compromise

- Suspicious overseas login
- Mimikatz credential harvesting
- .orionlock encrypted files

### Business Impact

- Operational disruption
- Financial impact
- Legal considerations
- Reputational damage

### Recommendations

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Security awareness training
- Email filtering
- Improved monitoring
- Offline backups

### Skills Practised

- Incident Response
- Risk Assessment
- Security Operations
- Threat Analysis
- Problem Solving

## Reflection

This exercise helped me understand how cybersecurity analysts assess breaches, identify vulnerabilities, evaluate business impact and recommend appropriate security controls.
